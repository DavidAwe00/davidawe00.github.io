Your plan and your information.

You can calculate a plan without creating an account. Account services, AI, and enrollment are only available where the operator has enabled them.

On this device

Your numbers stay in the page until you choose “Save on this device” or save a weekly check-in. Those actions store your plan and check-in history in this browser’s local storage. Other people using the same browser profile can see them. “Clear this device’s plan” removes that saved plan. Downloads are separate files that you control.

The education site also stores lesson progress and theme preferences locally. Clearing your plan does not clear lesson progress.

On the lessons, games, and tools

The rest of MoneyExplained works without an account and without cookies. Everything it remembers stays in this browser’s local storage: lesson progress and quiz scores, XP, daily-challenge streaks and badges, game high scores, your certificate name, the light/dark theme, which homepage sections you opened, anything you enter in the Savings Goals, Subscription Audit, and Net Worth tools, and your “Was this helpful?” answers (so you aren’t asked twice). None of it is sent to MoneyExplained unless you sign in to an account (see “Accounts” below). You can export and import your progress from the My progress page, and clearing your browser’s site data erases all of it.

The site doesn’t use analytics or advertising trackers.The site counts page views with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and doesn’t follow you across websites. Each count records the page’s path (never the address details after it, and never the planner page), the referring site, browser, screen size, and an approximate country; your IP address is used for that and not stored. If you answer “Was this helpful?” on a lesson or tool, GoatCounter counts that answer the same way: which lesson or tool, and yes or no. Nothing is counted if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. There are no advertising trackers. Like any website, loading pages involves a few outside services that see standard request information such as your IP address and browser type: the host (GitHub Pages, or the operator’s app host), Google Fonts for the typefaces, and cdnjs (Cloudflare) for the 3D coin animation on the homepage and the piggy-bank game. Their privacy policies apply to that information: GitHub, Google Fonts, Cloudflare. MoneyExplained videos are hosted on YouTube. Pages show our own preview image, and nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play; the player then comes from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google’s privacy policy applies to what you watch there.

Classes

If a teacher gives you a class code, you can join their class from My progress with a nickname. No account, email, or name is needed; please use something like a first name and last initial. While you’re in a class, MoneyExplained stores your nickname, which lessons you’ve finished with your quiz scores, and your Money Check-up scores (not your answers), and shows them to that teacher. Your lesson quiz answers are also sent as you choose them, so MoneyExplained can check your first-try score; your teacher sees that score, not the individual answers. If you move to a new device, your teacher can give you a one-time rejoin code that works for 24 hours. If your teacher assigns lessons, your browser asks the class for that list now and then and shows it on My progress. Your browser keeps a random key that links it to the class; MoneyExplained stores only a scrambled version of that key. Leaving the class, the teacher removing you, or the teacher deleting the class erases that record. Your progress on your own device isn’t affected. Teachers sign in with an adult account to see their classes.

Accounts

Accounts are optional and free. Anyone 13 or older can create one where account services are enabled; you don't need one to use any lesson, game, or tool. Children under 13 can't create an account. They can still use all of MoneyExplained, and join a class with a code from their teacher, which collects no email or name. When you sign up, we ask whether you're 18 or older or 13 to 17 and record that answer with your account. If you're 13 to 17, check with a parent or guardian before you sign up. Student (13 to 17) accounts sync learning progress and can save a plan; making classes, connecting a school's LMS or Google Classroom, AI coaching, and payments are for adult accounts.

Supabase handles your email, password authentication, and anything you save to your account. Session cookies are HttpOnly and are used to keep you signed in; no financial data is stored in them. While you're signed in, your learning progress is saved to your account automatically so it's on every device you sign in on: lesson quiz scores, XP, game high scores, daily-challenge streak, and your Money Check-up and capstone results. Your certificate name and the figures you type into calculators and tools stay in your browser. Saving your First Paycheck Plan to your account is a separate, explicit action. When you sign out, you can also remove your progress from that browser, which is a good idea on a shared or school computer.

“Delete my account” on the Account page deletes your login and everything stored with it: synced progress, a saved plan, and any classes you made. Payment records are kept as the law requires. Progress saved in your browser stays until you clear it. Provider backups may retain prior copies for a limited time under the operator's backup schedule.

Cloud plans

Saving your plan to your account and loading it back are explicit actions; local plan changes are not automatically uploaded.

“Delete my cloud plan” removes the stored profile and check-ins from the application database. It does not delete your login, payment records, browser copies, downloads, or provider backups. Cloud plans remain until you delete them or the operator removes the account. Provider backups may retain prior copies under the operator’s backup schedule.

AI explanations

Only when you check the consent box and request coaching does MoneyExplained send plan figures, up to four recent check-ins, and your question to the AI provider this site is configured to use, either OpenAI or Anthropic; the consent box names which one. Debt nicknames are replaced with generic labels. Account emails, passwords, and payment details are not included. Avoid adding names, account numbers, or other identifying details to your question.

OpenAI requests use the API’s store: false setting. Anthropic has no per-request equivalent; its standard API data-retention policy applies. Neither is a promise of zero provider retention: provider abuse monitoring and applicable retention policies can still apply. Responses are displayed in the page and are not saved with your cloud plan. Request timestamps are stored to enforce coaching limits. The operator can review usage counts, not chat text through this application.

AI can make mistakes. Figures come from deterministic calculators; explanations are educational and do not execute transfers or select investments.

Payments and email interest

When enrollment opens, Stripe hosts card entry. MoneyExplained stores payment identifiers, account association, and the access end date; it does not receive your full card number. The program is a one-time $29 USD purchase for 30 days with no automatic renewal. Refunded or disputed payments revoke program access.

The optional pilot-interest form stores your email and consent time in Supabase. It is a launch-interest list, not an active weekly newsletter. The app does not send marketing email itself. The operator must configure email delivery and an unsubscribe process before sending updates.

Operating this pilot

This page describes the application’s behavior. Public enrollment must remain closed until the operator publishes their identity, support contact, refund terms, retention schedule, and a process for account and email-list deletion. No live service configuration is implied by this page.
