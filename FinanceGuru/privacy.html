Your plan and your information.

You can calculate a plan without creating an account. Account services, AI, and enrollment are only available where the operator has enabled them.

On this device

Your numbers stay in the page until you choose “Save on this device” or save a weekly check-in. Those actions store your plan and check-in history in this browser’s local storage. Other people using the same browser profile can see them. “Clear this device’s plan” removes that saved plan. Downloads are separate files that you control.

The education site also stores lesson progress and theme preferences locally. Clearing your plan does not clear lesson progress.

Accounts and cloud plans

Account creation is for adults 18 and older. When account services are enabled, Supabase handles your email, password authentication, saved plan, and check-ins. Session cookies are HttpOnly and are used to keep you signed in. Financial data is not stored in those cookies. Saving to your account and loading from your account are explicit actions; local changes are not automatically uploaded.

“Delete my cloud plan” removes the stored profile and check-ins from the application database. It does not delete your login, payment records, browser copies, downloads, or provider backups. Cloud plans remain until you delete them or the operator removes the account. Provider backups may retain prior copies under the operator’s backup schedule.

AI explanations

Only when you check the consent box and request coaching does FinanceGuru send plan figures, up to four recent check-ins, and your question to OpenAI. Debt nicknames are replaced with generic labels. Account emails, passwords, and payment details are not included. Avoid adding names, account numbers, or other identifying details to your question.

Requests use the API’s store: false setting. This does not promise zero provider retention: provider abuse monitoring and applicable retention policies can still apply. Responses are displayed in the page and are not saved with your cloud plan. Request timestamps are stored to enforce coaching limits. The operator can review usage counts, not chat text through this application.

AI can make mistakes. Figures come from deterministic calculators; explanations are educational and do not execute transfers or select investments.

Payments and email interest

When enrollment opens, Stripe hosts card entry. FinanceGuru stores payment identifiers, account association, and the access end date; it does not receive your full card number. The program is a one-time $29 USD purchase for 30 days with no automatic renewal. Refunded or disputed payments revoke program access.

The optional pilot-interest form stores your email and consent time in Supabase. It is a launch-interest list, not an active weekly newsletter. The app does not send marketing email itself. The operator must configure email delivery and an unsubscribe process before sending updates.

Operating this pilot

This page describes the application’s behavior. Public enrollment must remain closed until the operator publishes their identity, support contact, refund terms, retention schedule, and a process for account and email-list deletion. No live service configuration is implied by this page.
