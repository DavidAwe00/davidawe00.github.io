Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

For students, parents, and teachers. More on the About page.

Is MoneyExplained really free?

Yes. The lessons, quizzes, games, calculators, the First Paycheck Plan, and the teacher materials are free to use, and there are no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress and plans are saved in your browser on this device. You can back up your progress from My progress and restore it on another device.

Where do the numbers come from?

From official and well-known sources such as the IRS, the Federal Reserve, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the FTC, and the U.S. Department of Education. Each lesson names its sources, and the figures are refreshed on a yearly schedule.

Is my information private?

What you enter stays in your browser unless you choose to join a class or create an account. The Privacy page lists exactly what is stored and where.

Can teachers use it in class?

Yes. Teachers & parents has a free lesson plan, printable worksheet with answer key, and slide deck for every lesson, plus a before-and-after Money Check-up to measure growth.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. Every lesson and quiz, the daily challenge, the Money Check-up, the tools, the games, and the five topic hubs are available in Spanish. The First Paycheck Plan and a few other pages are in English for now.

Is this financial advice?

No. MoneyExplained is education to help you understand your options. For decisions about your own situation, talk with a qualified professional.
