About FinanceGuru

Free, interactive money lessons for teens and new earners, and ready-to-run materials for the adults who teach them.

What it is

FinanceGuru has 20 short lessons with quizzes (in English and Spanish), games that turn money decisions into practice, calculators for real choices like a first paycheck, a car loan, or a job offer, and a free First Paycheck Plan. Teachers get 45-minute lesson plans, slides, printable worksheets, and a pre/post Money Check-up. Everything works without an account.

How facts are sourced and kept current

Every number comes from a public source named next to it, such as the IRS, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Federal Reserve, the FDIC, the CFPB, or KFF, with the date it applies to. Figures that change on a schedule (tax brackets, contribution limits, loan rates, prices) are refreshed on a yearly calendar when each source publishes. Calculators use simplified assumptions, which each tool explains.

FinanceGuru is educational. It isn't financial, tax, legal, or investment advice; see the Terms of Use.

Your information

Lessons, games, and tools save progress in your own browser. Students don't create accounts and no student data is collected. The Privacy & Data page has the details.

Contact and error reports

Spotted a number that's out of date, a broken page, or something unclear? Email FinanceGuru. Every page also has a "Report an error" link at the bottom that fills in the page for you. If you have a source for the correct figure, please include it.
