Your classes

Using Canvas, Schoology, or Moodle?

If your school has connected MoneyExplained, add it from your course: create an assignment, choose External tool (or the apps menu), and pick MoneyExplained. The first time, you connect the course to one of your classes. After that, students who open the assignment join the class automatically (no code), and their quiz scores go to your gradebook.

Not connected yet? Ask your LMS admin to add MoneyExplained using the setup page at .

Students see exactly what's shared before they join and can leave at any time. Ask students to use a first name and last initial, never a full name. Removing a student or deleting a class erases that progress from MoneyExplained.
