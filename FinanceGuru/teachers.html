How a class runs

  1. Warm-up (5 min). Pose the prompt and take a few answers.
  2. Lesson (10 min). Students open the lesson link on any device (Chromebooks and phones work) and finish the reading and the 3-question quiz.
  3. Activity (20 min). Pairs work through the tool or game and record their results.
  4. Discuss + exit ticket (10 min). Debrief, then use the printable quiz as an exit ticket.

Progress and XP are saved in each student's browser only. Every lesson is also available in Spanish (learn.html?lang=es) for English learners, with progress shared across both languages. Students who finish every lesson can print a FinanceGuru Fundamentals certificate to turn in.
