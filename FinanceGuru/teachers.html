How a class runs

  1. Project the slides. Every lesson has a ready-made deck (“📽 Present slides” on each plan, or all decks) that walks through the steps below, with quiz answers you reveal one at a time.
  2. Warm-up (5 min). Pose the prompt and take a few answers.
  3. Lesson (10 min). Students open the lesson link on any device (Chromebooks and phones work) and finish the reading and the 3-question quiz.
  4. Activity (20 min). Pairs work through the tool or game and record their results.
  5. Discuss + exit ticket (10 min). Debrief, then use the printable quiz as an exit ticket.
  6. No devices? Print the student worksheet: guided notes from the lesson text with a word bank, discussion questions with room to write, the quiz, and an answer key.

Progress and XP are saved in each student's browser only. Every lesson and the Money Check-up are also available in Spanish (learn.html?lang=es, checkup.html?lang=es) for English learners, with progress shared across both languages. Students who finish every lesson can print a FinanceGuru Fundamentals certificate to turn in.

Measure growth: the Money Check-up is a 10-question pre/post test (two questions per unit). Students take it on day one, then again at the end; the results screen shows before → after scores by unit for them to screenshot or show you. Answers stay hidden after the "before" check-up so the comparison is fair.

Capstone: Your First Year After Graduation (about 15 minutes) asks students to make seven decisions on a $50,000 salary: housing, transport, 401(k), savings account, spending, a first credit card, and a bonus. It then simulates twelve months and grades each unit. A good final-day activity: students play once, change one decision, and explain in writing which choice moved their net worth the most and why.
