Your Emergency Fund

The cushion that stops a bad week from becoming a bad year.

Start the interactive lesson →Leer en español →

Why you need one

Cars break. Jobs end. Teeth crack. An emergency fund is cash set aside only for surprises like these.

Without it, emergencies go on a credit card — and high interest turns a $1,000 problem into a $1,300 one.

How much to save

  • Starter goal: $1,000 or one month of expenses.
  • Full goal: 3–6 months of essential expenses.
  • Lean toward 6+ months if your income is irregular or you support others.
Try it: the Emergency Fund calculator finds your target.

Where to keep it

Somewhere safe and easy to reach — but not too easy. A high-yield savings account is ideal: it earns interest and is separate from your everyday checking.

Don't invest your emergency fund in stocks. It needs to be there when the market is down, too.
