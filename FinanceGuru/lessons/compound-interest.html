The Magic of Compound Interest

Why starting early matters more than starting big.

Start the interactive lesson →Leer en español →

Interest on your interest

Compound interest means you earn returns on your original money and on the returns it already earned.

$1,000 growing 8% a year becomes $1,080, then $1,166, then $1,260... The growth itself keeps growing.

Time is the secret ingredient

Meet two savers, both earning 8% a year:

  • Alex invests $200/month from age 25 to 35, then stops. Total put in: $24,000.
  • Sam invests $200/month from age 35 to 65. Total put in: $72,000.

At 65, Alex has more — about $400,000 vs Sam's ~$298,000 — despite investing a third as much. Alex's money simply had more time.

The Rule of 72

Quick mental math: divide 72 by your annual return to estimate how many years it takes to double your money.

At 8%, money doubles about every 9 years (72 ÷ 8). At 6%, every 12 years.
