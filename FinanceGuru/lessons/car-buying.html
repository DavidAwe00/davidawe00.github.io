Buying Your First Car

The real cost of a car, new vs used, and how to not get burned on the loan.

Start the interactive lesson →Leer en español →

The price tag is just the start

AAA estimates that owning and driving a new car costs about $12,863 a year (about $1,072 a month) once you add up everything for 15,000 miles of driving.

The biggest cost isn't gas. It's depreciation, the value the car loses, at about $4,422 a year. Insurance averages about $2,098 a year, and young drivers usually pay more.

Source: AAA, Your Driving Costs 2026.

New vs. used

In September 2026, the average new car sold for $50,562 (Kelley Blue Book). The average used car was listed at $27,235 (Cox Automotive).

New cars lose value fastest in their first few years, so a reliable car a few years old often gives you the most for your money. Before buying used:

  • Get a pre-purchase inspection from an independent mechanic.
  • Check the vehicle history report and look up open recalls by VIN at NHTSA.gov.

The loan can cost more than you think

New-car loans averaged about 7% APR in Q3 2026, with an average payment of $787 a month, and a record 1 in 4 new-car loans ran 84 months (7 years) or longer (Edmunds). Used-car loans usually have higher rates.

  • Get pre-approved at a bank or credit union before you shop, so you know your rate and can compare it with the dealer's offer.
  • Negotiate the out-the-door price, not the monthly payment. A lower payment on a longer loan can cost thousands more.
  • A common guideline is 20/4/10: 20% down, a loan of 4 years or less, and total car costs under 10% of your gross income.
Run the numbers: the Car Cost calculator compares loan lengths and your all-in monthly cost.

Should you lease?

A lease is a long-term rental: lower payments, but you don't own anything at the end. Leases limit how many miles you drive each year and charge for extra miles and wear.

Leasing can make sense if you want a new car every few years and drive predictably. If you want the lowest long-term cost, buying a reliable car and keeping it for many years usually wins.
