Beat the Book

Pick a bet size and a bet (or skip) each round. Keys: 1–4 to bet, ← → for bet size. XP is the same whether you win or lose.

A math simulation, not real betting. Each game is treated as a fair coin flip, so the only edge comes from how the bets are priced (−110 singles, a 4-leg parlay at about 12.3-to-1, and +500 on a 1-in-7 shot). Real sportsbooks kept about 10% of all money bet in 2025 (American Gaming Association). Need help? Call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (National Problem Gambling Helpline).
